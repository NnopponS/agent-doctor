# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Standards

- Focus on technical evidence, reproducible test cases, and constructive code review.
- Respect differing perspectives and experiences.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and learn from feedback.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to `worapon.sangs@gmail.com`.
